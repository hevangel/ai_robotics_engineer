## Addendum — HW-0269 identified (2026-11-23)

At the owner's direction ("look at the repos of git-hevangel — it has forked the upstream repo of that nixie clock"), the clock was matched to the **Nick2 Nixie Clock** project: the owner's GitHub account `git-hevangel` holds a mirror of **mcer12/Nick2-Nixie-Clock** (upstream description: "Nixie clocks based on ESP32 / ESP8266 and NTP"). The project ships IN-16, IN-12 and ZM1040 variants; close inspection of the photo shows honeycomb-mesh tubes with large stacked digit cathodes and **wire flying leads** at the glass pinch bases (no plastic pin base), which matches the project's **IN-12 version** (wire-lead form, consistent with IN-12B). The blue case with black top plate matches the project's 3D-printed cover. HW-0269 was renamed accordingly and its confidence raised from medium to high; design details (ESP8266/ESP32 + NTP over WiFi, direct drive, WS2812 LED colons, USB-C 5 V power) are recorded from the project documentation, not from testing the unit.
