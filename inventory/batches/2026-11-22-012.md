That board is **HW-0083**, a child of the traffic-light assembly, not a loose stock board. Total **five physical Arduino Nano-format boards** in this batch: `1 installed + 4 loose`. The screw-terminal adapter **HW-0084** is empty and is *not* counted as a sixth Nano.
