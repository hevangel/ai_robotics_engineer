## Duplicate and counting safeguards

- The **Raspberry Pi Pico W** (HW-0071) is a new RP2040 microcontroller board, not another Linux-class SBC or a substitute for HW-0073.
- The **FT2232H Mini Module** (HW-0072) is a USB interface/breakout module, not a complete MCU computer.
- The **Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W** (HW-0073) is one newly visible board, separate from earlier photographed Pi 3/Pi 4/Pi 5 boards.
- The **ESP32-WROOM-32** dev board (HW-0075) is a different physical module from the packaged ESP32-S3 Waveshare display board HW-0064.
- The **GPIO extension adapter** (HW-0076) is a bare passive pin breakout; it is not another single-board computer and its cable is not confirmed.
- The **Smraza Pi 4 case** (HW-0077) contains case parts and hardware but **no visible Raspberry Pi computer**; don't increase the owned Pi 4 board count.
- The **DIYmall micro:bit expansion board** (HW-0078) is a micro:bit accessory, **not a fourth micro:bit**. Prior user confirmation remains **3 actual BBC micro:bit boards**.
