## Images and identifications

- `P-20261122-179` — `1000063328.jpg`: Small purple barometric breakout, front side. Silkscreen `BME/BMP280`; header `VIN / GND / SCL / SDA`; one mounting hole; metal-capped sensor with round pressure port and a faint laser marking that stays illegible at 2× enlargement. → HW-0208. Board family certain; BME280 vs BMP280 undetermined (the board's own silkscreen leaves it open too), medium confidence.
- `P-20261122-180` — `1000063329.jpg`: Tiny purple laser ranging module. Silkscreen `GYVL53L0K` (the VL53L0X designation; the final X is rendered narrowly); black optical sensor package with two apertures; header `VIN / GND / SCL / SDA` plus two unlabelled pins in the XSHUT/GPIO1 positions; one mounting hole. → HW-0209, high confidence: ST VL53L0X time-of-flight distance sensor on the GY-VL53L0X breakout.
- `P-20261122-181` — `1000063330.jpg`: Small dark-blue breakout, back side only. Silkscreen `GY-271`; header `VCC / GND / SCL / SDA / DRDY`. → HW-0210. GY-271 is the standard HMC5883L 3-axis magnetometer/compass module; some GY-271 boards carry the pin-compatible QMC5883L instead, and only a front photo or a device-ID read can tell them apart — medium confidence.
