## Key deduplication and uncertainty notes

- **Photo 1000063284:** Exactly 3 small light actuator tactile buttons and 6 much larger black-centered square switch mechanisms. Larger switches' caps/compatible button style unknown. A cropped corner of an LCD shield at upper-right appears to be existing **HW-0151**; NOT an additional shield.
- **Photo 1000063290:** One 4×4 membrane keypad, sixteen marked keys, roughly 8 contacts on the flat ribbon connector.
- **Photo 1000063291:** One glass fuse in a label bag reading **T3.15A**. The label describes a time-delay 3.15A fuse in a voltage selector warning context, but body rating and voltage not independently verified. There appears also to be a thin loose metal piece; identity not established or separately counted.
- **Photo 1000063292:** One Keyes-marked rotary encoder board with five printed pins **CLK, DT, SW, +, GND**. KY-040 type; exact model SKU and electrical behavior untested.
- **Photo 1000063293:** **Five** potentiometers, with visible labels in photo order **B100K, B10K, B50K, B10K, B100K**. Separate records for each resistance: 2 / 2 / 1. B-marking usually implies linear taper, but not tested.
- **Photo 1000063294:** One CanaKit PiSwitch with ON/OFF button, indicator lens and integral short cable. No assumption of safe-shutdown circuit, electrical power rating, or definite USB connector type from this photo.
- **Photo 1000063295:** One Olympus **VF-RM7** wired remote; **REC/PAUSE** function is printed on the case. Cable included and not extra inventory.
- **Photos 1000063296 and 1000063297:** Front and rear of the **same one** white Apple Remote. Back clearly marked **Model No. A1156**. The serial number is not copied into this document.
