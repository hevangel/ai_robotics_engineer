## Identification and deduplication notes

- **Clear LEDs:** six visible transparent-lens through-hole parts in photo 1000063276.jpg. Emitted light color is *unknown* from clear lens appearance; some clear packages may differ in size and chip type.
- **Yellow and green LEDs:** 3 yellow-bodied and 5 green-bodied through-hole parts. These are individual loose parts and not extra copies of the finished traffic-light modules recorded earlier.
- **Red LEDs:** approximately 18 red-bodied parts with some overlapping leads/components. One at upper right is noticeably smaller than the remaining red cluster. Count is photographic estimate, not an independently sorted unit count.
- **Buzzer:** one black round two-terminal-style sounder. A visible sound hole does not establish whether its electronics self-oscillate; operating voltage, active/passive classification and model are NOT readable and must not be guessed.
- **WS2812 breakouts:** four red breakout PCBs clearly labelled `WS2812 Breakout`, each carrying one 5050-class RGB LED and `5V/DI/DO/GND` labeled input/output pads. Exact LED revision and functionality unverified. These are distinct from LED matrix modules HW-0117, HW-0118, HW-0130–HW-0132.
- **Ten-segment bar-graph packages:** photo 1000063272.jpg shows ten bar-graph packages arranged 2 × 5 with foam between. Previously recorded 1000063264.jpg showed ten nearly identical components in 2 × 5 arrangement. **Conservatively treat new photograph as alternate view of existing ten (HW-0133), not ten more.** Physical identity not serial-verified.
- **One-digit seven-segment packages:** 20 (4 rows × 5), each showing a single numeral plus decimal point. These differ from five loose four-digit packages HW-0138 and driver breakouts HW-0134–HW-0136. Model, common pinout and display color unknown.
