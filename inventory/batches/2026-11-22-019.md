## Distinctions and deduplication

- **8×8 panels, style C (HW-0130):** two 8×8 panels with separately framed LED packages and round lens windows. Unlike the four-section blue PCB chains already inventoried as styles A and B, no PCB reverse or control chip is shown. 2 physical panels in one photo.
- **Bare 8×8 modules (HW-0131):** two plain black-faced dot-matrix packages are together in 1000063262.jpg. A similar **single** bare module in 1000063268.jpg might be a third or one of the same two. Record 2 confirmed; the additional view does not justify a third unit until identity resolved. Not part of the HW-0117/HW-0118 chained assemblies.
- **Driven 8×8 module (HW-0132):** one LED matrix is shown installed on a MAX7219CNG controller board with 5 Dupont wires. Count as one assembled device, not an additional bare LED module.
- **Four-digit LED modules (HW-0134 and HW-0135):** four assembled PCBs have 4-pin male headers; upper two with plain DM silkscreen, lower two with a large “4-DigitLED Display” title. Separate two physical module styles even if their actual driver IC later proves identical.
- **Headerless four-digit module (HW-0136):** photo 1000063270.jpg has an empty connector footprint. Distinct-looking from populated-header boards, but **may** be a rephoto of one of the lower two boards if the header is on another side or hidden; count 1 provisionally and flag for owner review. No electrical interface assumption.
- **Bare four-digit displays (HW-0138):** five loose components with no breakout boards. Different from any assembled driver module.
- **Traffic lights (HW-0137):** 3 independent factory-made black PCB modules. HW-0082 is the user's separate Arduino Nano DIY traffic-light assembly.
- **RFID/NFC labels (HW-0129):** white square labels with visible concentric coil. Two piles plus an isolated example; number of individual labels concealed, model, frequency and protocol unverified. Quantity kept null.
- **Ten-segment bar graphs (HW-0133):** 10 distinct 10-segment components, not four-digit or 8×8 modules.
