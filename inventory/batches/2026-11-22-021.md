## Inventory details and deduplication

- **Dupont leads:** `1000063277.jpg` and the lower bundle in `1000063278.jpg` most likely show the SAME tied male-to-male jumper set, HW-0148, recorded as 1 bundle. The upper bundle in photo 3278 is HW-0149, recorded separately as 1 bundle; black housing and exposed pins appear to be mixed, but exact per-wire male/female connectors remain uncertain. Existing female-to-female bundle HW-0091, preformed rigid wires HW-0103, and other related cable entries remain unchanged.
- **Hook probes HW-0150:** One mixed-color miniature IC-hook probe lead set; physical identity relative to black-hook logic analyzer leads HW-0039 and bagged hook accessories HW-0042 is not serial confirmed, but visual distinction supports a separate record.
- **LCD Keypad Shield HW-0151:** One Arduino format board including 16x2 LCD, contrast trimmer, six black pushbuttons. Do not inventory the integrated LCD/buttons separately.
- **Red-foam parts photo HW-0152/HW-0153/HW-0154:** One CTS 208-4 four-position DIP switch; one small metal-framed slide switch (tentative type); one small blue Keyes/WiiChuck adapter board. The same image also shows a yellow four-bar LED display and green seven-bar display, associated tentatively with existing HW-0146/HW-0147. DO NOT ADD EXTRA UNITS for those two displays without evidence that they are distinct physical devices.
- **Plastic caps HW-0155:** Exactly 15 loose caps, 3 black, 3 green, 3 blue, 3 white, 3 yellow. These are passive accessories, not electronic switches. Compatibility not established.
- **Thumbsticks:** Photo 3288 shows two complete loose 5-pin analog joystick breakout modules (HW-0156). Photo 3287 shows one red Funduino Joystick Shield v1.A (HW-0157) with a THIRD joystick physically installed on the shield, four big blue/yellow buttons, two small buttons. Count one complete shield, not a third loose thumbstick. NRF24L01 header printed, module not installed.
- **Red pushbuttons HW-0158:** Four separate panel-mount red buttons with green wires pictured, some leads possibly loose. Contact type (momentary/latching; NO/NC) and electrical ratings unknown.
- **No functional tests** are shown in any photograph. The uploaded image binaries were not transferred to GitHub, so every photo intake row has `asset_status: not_stored`, with original filename and exact associated hardware IDs retained.
